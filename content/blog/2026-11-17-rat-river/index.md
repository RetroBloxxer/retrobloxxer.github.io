## M4 (Metro) | Kadıköy - Sabiha Gökçen Havalimanı
<image src="M4.jpg" class="no-hover"/>

this metro starts from Kadıköy and ends in the Sabiha Gökçen Havalimanı, one of the most popular airports in turkey.

the stops are in order:
- Kadıköy
- Ayrılık Çeşmesi (infill opened at October 29, 2013) (transfer to B1)
- Acıbadem
- Ünalan (transfer to Metrobus)
- Göztepe
- Yenisahra
- Kozyatağı (transfer to M8)
- Bostancı
- Küçükyalı
- Maltepe
- Huzurevi
- Gülsuyu
- Esenkent
- Hastane-Adliye
- Soğanlık
- Kartal (terminal stop during 2012-2016)
- Yakacık-Adnan Kahveci
- Pendik
- Tavşantepe (terminal stop during 2016-2022)
- Fevzi Çakmak-Hastane
- Yayalar-Şehli
- Kurtköy
- Sabiha Gökçen Havalimanı (terminal stop)

the metro was first concepted at 2002, with the initial concept being from Harem to Tuzla.
construction was started at 2004, and the metro line was finished in 2012; from Kadıköy to Kartal.
The Ayrılık Çeşmesi station was added in October 29, 2013; as an infill to connect the B1 line and the M4 line.
The Tavşantepe extension was made in 2016 with 4 new stations being put.
The SAW extension was brought in 2022 with 4 new stations including the airport.

i'd rate this metro a 7/10 because its smooth and its kind of the only way me and my mom can get to the airport without having to get a taxi

## B1 (Marmaray) | Halkalı - Gebze
<image src="B1.jpg" class="no-hover"/>

this suburban line starts from Halkalı and ends in Gebze, essentially WRAPPING around a whole continent all the way from the europe side to the asia side.

the stops are in order:
- Halkalı (transfer to YHT and regional train)
- Mustafa Kemal
- Küçükçekmece (transfer to Metrobus)
- Florya
- Florya Akvaryum
- Yeşilköy
- Yeşilyurt
- Ataköy (transfer to M9)
- Bakırköy (transfer to M3 and YHT)
- Yenimahalle
- Zeytinburnu (transfer to Metrobus and T1)
- Kazlıçeşme (transfer to U3 / T6)
- Yenikapı (transfer to M1A, M1B, M2 and IDO)
- Sirkeci (terminal stop for B1 before merge) (transfer to T1 and U3 / T6)
B2 (Asia):
- Üsküdar (transfer to M5 and Ferries)
- Ayrılık Çeşmesi (transfer to M4)
- Söğütlüçeşme (transfer to Metrobus and YHT)
- Feneryolu
- Göztepe
- Erenköy
- Suadiye
- Bostancı (transfer to M8, YHT and Ferries)
- Küçükyalı
- İdealtepe
- Süreyya Plaji
- Maltepe
- Cevizli
- Atalar
- Başak
- Kartal (not the M4's kartal stop)
- Yunus
- Pendik (transfer to regional train and YHT)
- Kaynarca
- Tersane
- Güzelyalı
- Aydıntepe
- İçmeler
- Tuzla
- Çayırova
- Fatih
- Osmangazi
- Darıca
- Gebze (terminal stop for B2 and B1 after merge) (transfer to YHT and regional train)

the line was released in 1955 with B1 being Sirkeci - Halkalı and B2 being Haydarpaşa - Gebze.
in 2013, B1 and B2 were closed to merge the lines together with the tunnel of Ayrılık Çeşmesi - Kazlıçeşme being opened on the same year.
in 2019, B1 and B2 were merged and is now coded B1.
the old Sirkeci - Kazlıçeşme line was restored and officially coded U3 / T6 after 2024.

i'd rate this suburban line a 8.5/10 due to it being very unique (a HUGE number of stops) and the way we can get to the M4 and other stuff

## M5 (Metro) | Üsküdar - Sultanbeyli
<image src="M5.jpg" class="no-hover"/>

this metro line is one of the most unique ones due to it being the first unattended train to ever exist in turkey.
the line starts at Üsküdar and ends in Sultanbeyli.

the stops are in order:
- Üsküdar (transfer to Marmaray and Ferries)
- Fıstıkağıcı
- Bağlarbaşı
- Altunizade (transfer to Metrobus and currently WIP M14)
- Kısıklı
- Bulgurlu
- Ümraniye
- Çarşı (transfer to currently finished but unreleased M12)
- Yamanevler (terminal stop during 2012-2017)
- Çakmak
- Ihlamurkuyu
- Altınşehir
- İmam Hatip Lisesi
- Dudullu (transfer to M8)
- Necip Fazıl (transfer to ????)
- Çekmeköy (terminal station during 2017-2024)
- Meclis
- Sarıgazi (transfer to currently WIP M13)
- Sancaktepe
- Samandıra Merkez (terminal stop during 2024-2026)
- Sancaktepe Şehir Hastanesi
- Veysel Karani
- Hasanpaşa
- Sultanbeyli

the metro was first concepted at 2009 as a standard rail to Şile and etc.
construction for M5 first began in 2012 and during this time, the munincipality decided to upgrade signal technology giving landmark to the first unattended train in turkey.
the M5 was released in 2017 with the driverless train actually working.
the Çekmeköy extension was released in 2018 with 7 new stops being put.
the Samandıra Merkez extension was released in 2024 with 4 new stops being put.
the Sultanbeyli extension was released in May 22 of this year with 4 new stops being put.

i'd rate this a 9/10 due to it's personal uniqueness and etc even though i havent boarded it yet

## M9 (Metro) | Ataköy - Olimpiyat
<image src="M9.jpg" class="no-hover"/>

this metro line starts from Ataköy and ends in Olimpiyat.

the stops are in order:
- Ataköy (transfer to B1)
- Yenibosna (transfer to M1A and Metrobus)
- Çobançeşme
- 29 Ekim-Cumhuriyet
- Doğu Sanayi
- Mimar Sinan (transfer to currently WIP M1B extension)
- 15 Temmuz
- Halkalı Caddesi
- Atatürk Mahallesi (transfer to currently WIP M7 extension)
- Bahariye (starting stop during 2021-2024)
- MASKO
- İkitelli Sanayi (transfer to M3)
- Ziya Gökalp Mahallesi
- Olimpiyat (transfer to M11)

the metro line was first built in 2013, with it being a branch of the M3 metro.
construction of the Ataköy extension was started; due to financial issues, it had some delays but after the issues were solved, work was significantly faster.
the M9 was released first in 2021 with the İkitelli - Olimpiyat line being transferred to M9 along with the MASKO stop opening.
the Ataköy extension was finished with 8 stops being put and one stop being built later.

i'd rate this a 6/10, nothing interesting + i havent boarded this metro before
